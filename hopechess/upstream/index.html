HOW TO PLAY

Hopechess rules

Choose your moves in secret. Both moves resolve together. Capture the opposing king to win.

01

Simultaneous moves

Both players choose one legal move from the board as it stands. Tap a destination to submit; both moves happen together. In pass & play, confirm with Lock in move. There is no check.

02

Collisions

Two pieces land on one square? The weaker captures the stronger, even a pawn moving forward. Matching pieces remove each other.

WEAKEST → STRONGEST
03

Capturing pieces rest

A surviving piece that captures must rest for the entire next round. Look for the little pause marker. Equal pieces that collide both leave the board.

04

Capture the king

Capture their king to win. Kings may move into danger; check and checkmate don’t exist. A king may target the opposing king only if that king is resting from a capture in the previous round. If two active kings move to the same empty square, both return to their starting squares. Both kings captured by other pieces in one round? It’s a draw.

Special moves and draws +
  • Ordinary captures work normally against a piece that stays put. A piece that moves away escapes, including when pieces swap squares or cross paths.
  • Except for en passant, pawns may move diagonally only if an enemy is on that square at the start of the round. If it escapes, the pawn still completes its diagonal move.
  • Pieces block paths based on the starting board, even if they later move. Pawns move forward into initially empty squares and may advance two squares from their starting rank.
  • Pawns promote to a queen, rook, bishop, or knight upon reaching the last rank. The chosen promotion takes effect before captures and collisions, so a pawn promoting to a queen collides at queen strength.
  • Castle by moving the king two squares toward a rook. Neither piece may have moved or be resting, and all squares between them must be empty. Attacked squares do not matter. The king and rook move simultaneously; each can be captured at its destination independently.
  • En passant is available only in the round after an opposing pawn advances two squares. Move an adjacent pawn diagonally behind it. If the target moves again in that round, it escapes; your diagonal move still happens. The capturing pawn must survive any collision to capture en passant.
  • If a side has no legal move, it must pass. If neither side can move after resting pieces recover, the game is a draw.
  • In 2v2, two players share each color and submit one move each. Teammates must use different pieces and destinations; the first submitted move reserves them. Castling reserves both king and rook. Starting-board blockers still apply. If no distinct legal move remains, the other teammate passes automatically. All four orders resolve together with the usual collision, rest, promotion, and king-capture rules. Resigning or leaving an active game forfeits for your whole team. All four players must accept a rematch.
  • Spectators can watch any room by its party code, without taking a player seat. Submitted moves stay hidden until the round resolves.
  • Multiplayer games are rated by default, including guests. Turn off Rated game when creating a party to opt out. Each profile has separate 1v1 and 2v2 ratings, starting at 1,000 Elo, with a K-factor of 32. In 2v2, Elo uses each team’s average 2v2 rating and both teammates receive the same change. A profile can occupy only one seat in a rated party. Resigning, running out of time, or leaving after play begins counts as a loss.
  • The same position repeating three times, or 100 rounds without a pawn move or capture, is a draw.
